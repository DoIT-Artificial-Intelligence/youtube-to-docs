    Pricing

    youtube-to-docs is free and open source. You pay only your AI provider, at their list prices, and every run records its estimated cost in the output CSV. Prices last updated 2026-07-21.

    What a video costs

    The estimated cost of one summary pass over a video's transcript for each text model. Move the slider to match your video's length.

    How the estimate works

    A summary pass sends the whole transcript and gets about 2,000 tokens back. Transcripts run about 200 tokens per minute of speech; the benchmark video is 3h11m and 37,663 tokens. A full run makes several passes (speakers, Q&A, summary, one-sentence summary and tags), and Q&A also sends the timed captions, so a full text run costs roughly 4–5 times one pass. Claude models count about a third more tokens for the same text.

    Price list

    Units, conversions and sources
    • Token prices are USD per 1 million tokens. Cached input applies when the same prompt prefix is reused.
    • Speech-to-text, text-to-speech and translation services are shown in the vendor's own unit (per minute or per 1M characters). The CLI converts them to token equivalents (about 4 characters or 0.2 seconds of audio per token) to estimate costs.
    • The per hour estimates assume 150 spoken words (about 900 characters) a minute, and that Gemini audio uses 32 tokens per second.
    • Infographic costs were measured on a real run and include the image prompt.
    • Prices come from the vendors' pricing pages and llm-prices.com, and they live in youtube_to_docs/prices.py, which is the same file the CLI uses. Spot a stale price? Open an issue.
